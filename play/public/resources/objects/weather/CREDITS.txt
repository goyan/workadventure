Rain and thunder sound effects synthesized for this WorkAdventure fork.
Original procedural noise, filtering and envelopes; no sampled recordings.
Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

Rain: seamless eight-second mono loop. Thunder: six-second mono rumble.
Both are PCM WAV, 22050 Hz, 16-bit. Snow intentionally has no sound.
